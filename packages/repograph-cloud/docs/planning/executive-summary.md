## 🚧 Risks & Mitigation

### Technical Risks

**Risk:** Scaling indexing to 100K+ repos
- **Mitigation:** Incremental indexing, distributed workers, queue prioritization
- **Contingency:** Partner with Sourcegraph for enterprise indexing

**Risk:** Search performance degradation
- **Mitigation:** Elasticsearch optimization, caching, query optimization
- **Contingency:** Invest in infrastructure earlier than planned

### Business Risks

**Risk:** Sourcegraph drops prices to compete
- **Mitigation:** Emphasize AI-first features, modern UX, speed
- **Moat:** Deep AI integrations take months to replicate

**Risk:** GitHub/GitLab enhance built-in search
- **Mitigation:** Multi-platform advantage, graph analysis they lack
- **Moat:** We work across all platforms, they don't

**Risk:** Low willingness to pay
- **Mitigation:** Freemium model, clear ROI metrics (time saved)
- **Validation:** Interview 50 developers before MVP launch

### Market Risks

**Risk:** AI coding tools make search obsolete
- **Mitigation:** We become the context provider for AI tools
- **Opportunity:** More AI usage = more need for our context layer

**Risk:** Economic downturn, dev tool budgets cut
- **Mitigation:** Price point low enough ($199/mo) to survive
- **Strategy:** Prove ROI (saves 10+ hours/developer/month)
