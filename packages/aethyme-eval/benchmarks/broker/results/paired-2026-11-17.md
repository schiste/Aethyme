# Paired broker workflow comparison, 2026-11-17

Six Playground tasks ran in both arms with Codex `gpt-6-sol`, low reasoning
effort, and seed 19. The broker was Aethyme 0.8.120, commit
`079b61a4c67c210486ac236ce65c44d4d7979ea4`, binary SHA-256
`e54590064ea78f8b2ee3e76d5dc77a4e98ef8b3f879f4505d505ce39b9b4559b`.
This build predates the new lifecycle and attribution changes. The same
worktree isolation, allowed paths, validation, and local publication
requirements applied in both arms.

Changes divide the difference by the Git value. The median paired elapsed
difference was 12.0 seconds. All six broker runs took longer than their direct
match. Each arm completed 6 of 6 tasks, encountered 2 of 6 merge conflicts,
required rework on those two tasks, and used zero human interventions. Both
injected conflicts were repaired within the single allowed repair turn.

Nearest-rank p90 with six observations is the maximum. The uncached p90
change reflects t04's high baseline cache miss count. The peer was introduced
after publication, so these trials measure the cost of recovery on a shared
conflict. They do not estimate early duplicate-work prevention or production
overlap rates. Rerun with the same plan against the new broker binary to
measure the change in workflow cost.
