## Observations

1. **Deprecated Methods**: Two methods (`modifyWatchedItemsWithRCInfo` and `modifyWatchedItemsWithRCInfoQuery`) are explicitly marked as deprecated since version 1.46 and replaced with `ApiQueryBaseBeforeQueryHook`.

2. **Internal Utility Pattern**: Several methods follow an intentional pattern where a public method with rights checks calls an internal method without rights checks:
   - `removeWatch()` → `removeWatchIgnoringRights()`
   - `isTempWatched()` → `isTempWatchedIgnoringRights()`

3. **Testing Utility**: `overrideDeferredUpdatesAddCallableUpdateCallback()` appears to be a testing utility exposed publicly but never used in production code.

4. **Interface Implementations**: Despite being part of public interfaces (`WatchedItemStoreInterface`, `WatchedItemQueryServiceExtension`), several methods are never actually called from outside the directory, suggesting they may be:
   - Part of deprecated interfaces
   - Intended for future use
   - Requirements of interface contracts but not actually used
